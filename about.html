TextVision is an inclusive educational initiative designed to support visually impaired learners in achieving academic independence through literacy skill development. Sponsored by the University of Ilorin, Nigeria, under the University Senate Research Grant, the project reflects the university’s strong commitment to advancing accessibility and inclusive education for all learners. Led by Dr. Muhinat Bolanle Bello, Associate Professor of Sociology of Education and the Principal Researcher of the project, TextVision focuses on empowering visually impaired students through practical literacy programs such as Reading with Duxbury Reader and Writing with Raised-Line Paper. The project equips learners with the skills and confidence to read and write using specialized materials that enhance comprehension, creativity, and expression. It also provides facilitators with effective strategies and tools to deliver inclusive instruction tailored to individual learning needs. By bridging the literacy gap and fostering academic independence, TextVision is creating pathways for visually impaired students to thrive in their educational journeys and beyond. At its core, the project stands as a collaborative effort between researchers, facilitators, and learners, a shared vision to make education truly inclusive and accessible for all.
